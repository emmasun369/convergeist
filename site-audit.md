# ConvergeIST Current-Site Audit

## What the existing site communicates

ConvergeIST is a peer-led landing and arrival-support service for international students heading to China. Its strongest differentiator is a warm, familiar voice: rather than presenting as an impersonal travel agent, it positions the team as a reliable older sibling who has already navigated the same transition.

## Existing information architecture

| Area | Current message | Upgrade opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Anxiety around arrival, language, and unfamiliar systems | Lead with a clear promise, audience, and two decisive paths: explore support or start a plan |
| About | Student-led guidance and culturally empathetic support | Turn the positioning into a credibility-rich "why us" section with a clear operating model |
| Services | Housing, airport transport, local food, temporary accommodation, and money guidance | Organize services around a complete arrival journey and make inclusions actionable |
| Survey | A link to an intake form | Give the intake a dedicated conversion section with expectations, steps, and a transparent response window |
| Testimonials | Four personal accounts from former students | Do not reproduce user-generated statements without confirmed permission; reserve an expandable proof section for approved, verified stories |
| Lower-page content | Unrelated placeholder event blocks | Remove entirely and replace with resources, FAQs, and a final conversion section |

## Core strengths to retain

- The direct acknowledgement of culture shock, language barriers, food, transport, and housing uncertainty.
- A personable, supportive voice with lived-experience credibility.
- Specific, practical arrival services rather than abstract promises.
- The approachable Chinese-language motif, including a friendly \"欢迎来到中国\" moment.

## Priority improvements

1. **Clarify the offer.** State who ConvergeIST helps, when support starts, and what a student can expect in the first few days.
2. **Build a proper service journey.** Group services into pre-arrival, landing day, and settling-in phases instead of a flat list.
3. **Replace one-page ambiguity with conversion architecture.** Add a focused intake route, clear next steps, and a documented response expectation.
4. **Create trust without unsupported claims.** Use process transparency, service standards, and partner due-diligence language instead of invented metrics or reviews.
5. **Increase visual distinctiveness.** Move away from generic image blocks toward an editorial travel-notebook interface with an ownable wayfinding system.
6. **Finish the product surface.** Add responsive navigation, FAQ, resource previews, an accessible mobile menu, and polished interaction states.

## Implementation scope

The upgrade will be a high-fidelity static frontend prototype with an expansive home page and dedicated service, arrival-plan, and resource routes. Intake and contact entry points will be clearly surfaced; wiring to a chosen form, CRM, scheduling tool, or messaging channel can be added once those destinations are supplied.
