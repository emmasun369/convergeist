# Chosen Direction: Arrival Notebook

## Design Movement

**Contemporary editorial travel journal with East Asian wayfinding cues.** The site borrows the calm composure of a well-made city guide and the warmth of a handwritten arrival note, not stereotypical tourism imagery. It should feel like the helpful orientation pack a student wishes they received before their flight.

## Core Principles

1. **Orient before persuading:** Every section reduces uncertainty by putting a student on a clear path.
2. **Warmth through specificity:** Language, images, and microcopy should name real moments of transition rather than sell vague empowerment.
3. **Deliberate asymmetry:** Sections are composed like notes and route panels on a page, with offset content blocks and substantial breathing room.
4. **Trust through transparency:** What happens next, what is included, and what requires coordination should always be visible.

## Color Philosophy

The base is a warm rice-paper cream that lowers visual stress and gives long guidance content room to breathe. **Converge Jade** anchors navigation, service states, and decisive actions as a calm, directional color rather than a generic tech-blue. A muted cinnabar accent creates visual signals for moments of arrival, labels, and highlighted language, while ink-black typography preserves editorial clarity.

## Layout Paradigm

The interface is a **route map in pages**: a slim rail carries section markers and small journey labels, while wide content panes shift between editorial text, card clusters, image windows, and timeline components. The hero uses an off-axis composition: a text masthead starts the route while a visual “arrival card” overlaps the page edge. It avoids a single centered column and lets the reader travel through the content.

## Signature Elements

1. **Route lines and station dots** that thread through journey stages, service cards, and the footer.
2. **Annotated captions** that pair short Mandarin phrases with plain-English context.
3. **Stamped labels** such as “Before you fly,” “Landing day,” and “Your first week,” adding a purposeful editorial rhythm.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should confirm direction, not decorate. Links draw a small directional underline, route stations reveal a concise detail on focus or hover, and buttons feel like deliberate next steps. The mobile menu should make the arrival journey immediately scannable rather than hiding it behind a generic list.

## Animation

Elements enter with small, staggered upward fades and route lines subtly extend as their section appears. Cards raise by only a few pixels and deepen their shadow on hover. Travel direction should be signalled by left-to-right movement for progression; no looping motion, overlong scroll effects, or distracting parallax. All nonessential movement is disabled for users who prefer reduced motion.

## Typography System

**DM Serif Display** provides an editorial, empathetic voice for major headlines and Chinese welcome moments. **Manrope** handles guides, labels, controls, and long-form body copy with compact, legible proportions. Headline typography is large and left-aligned; body text is narrower, generous in leading, and never compressed into dense cards. Labels use Manrope in tracked uppercase at a small but readable size.

## Brand Essence

**ConvergeIST is the student-led arrival partner that turns a move to China into a well-guided first chapter.**

Personality: **resourceful, welcoming, clear-eyed.**

## Brand Voice

Copy is calm, concrete, and lightly conversational. Headlines name the moment a student is living through; calls to action promise the next practical action rather than issuing a generic command.

> “Your first day in China should feel like an arrival, not a test.”

> “Tell us where you’re landing. We’ll help map what comes next.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark is a custom-feeling **CONVERGEIST** setting with a small travelling dot that moves through the letterforms, echoed by a standalone abstract map-pin/route symbol: a looping jade line terminating in a cinnabar station dot. The mark is used at a legible size in the header and as the favicon.

## Signature Brand Color

**Converge Jade — #0D6D5D.** A saturated deep jade green that owns the directional, dependable quality of the brand.

## Style Decisions

Business-visitor pages remain part of the same **ConvergeIST arrival-route system**: use the language of route, handoff, landing, field note, and what comes next rather than presenting a separate logistics-consultancy identity. Images should feel like candid arrival documentation from China—meetings, notes, transit, supplier context, and practical paperwork—not generic corporate imagery or playful visual metaphors. **Converge Jade** remains the dominant memory color; cinnabar is reserved for stamps, station dots, warnings, and final arrival emphasis.

The business experience should feel like a prepared **China arrival packet**. Repeat bilingual micro-annotations, field-note labels, document-card overlaps, and the travelling-dot mark at practical decision points. Keep Cinnabar for stamps and stations; use jade for the directional surfaces that carry the route forward.
