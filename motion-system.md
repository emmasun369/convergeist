# ConvergeIST Motion System

## Narrative intent

Motion follows the student’s movement from uncertainty to orientation. It never competes with the content; it briefly reveals direction, confirms progress, or adds a little tactile physicality to the notes and tools that support the journey.

| Story beat | Visual behavior | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Departure becomes arrival | A small three-dimensional jade route ribbon travels toward a cinnabar station in the hero. | A distant trip becomes a visible, navigable path. |
| A path becomes understandable | The journey rail is drawn on scroll; each stage gains focus as it enters. | Support is a sequence of manageable steps. |
| An unfamiliar language becomes a welcome | The Chinese welcome line settles from a short route-like motion. | Context comes through connection, not memorisation. |
| An administrative task becomes progress | Arrival-form completion advances a visible route. | Sharing a plan is the first move, not a burden. |
| Guidance becomes an object to keep | Resource cards respond with a small, bounded tilt. | The field notes feel collected and personal. |

## Performance and accessibility constraints

The Three.js canvas is limited to the hero, uses a low device-pixel-ratio cap, pauses outside the viewport, and is omitted for reduced-motion users. Scroll animation is transform- and opacity-led, all interaction remains usable without a pointer, and mobile motion is deliberately more restrained than desktop motion.
